Yes — every package includes 90 days of complimentary support. After that, we offer maintenance plans scoped to how active your site needs to be.

Yes, we handle domain registration and hosting setup as part of onboarding, so your site is live and correctly configured from day one.

What if I need changes after the project is completed?+

Minor changes are covered under your 90-day support window. Beyond that, we'll scope a maintenance plan around your update frequency.
